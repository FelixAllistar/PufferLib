# Mode-2 automatic farm operation — 2026-09-05

Problem: structured animal production placed and maintained livestock but did
not harvest products; BUY_ANIMAL alone left purchased stock unplaced; HARVEST
excluded maintenance. A policy switching strategic actions could therefore
fail to realize the investment despite legal individual actions.

Mode 2 now shares automatic maintenance and harvesting across the structured
strategic actions. Already-owned animals are picked up and placed into existing
compatible empty housing on subsequent non-species actions, including HOLD.
An explicit species-production action still observes its requested batch cap.
Background pickup does not take a wheat-carrying feeder away from unfed animals.

Automatic one-shot crop harvest waits until maximum-yield age, except in the
last day. Ongoing crops and animals are collected when yield is available.
Explicit HARVEST can collect a younger legally harvestable crop, while keeping
maintenance enabled. The simulator still performs normal end-of-day inventory
transport. Products are not automatically sold by these actions. DIVERSIFY
retains its pre-existing delegated strategy, including sales.

PPO still chooses new investments, quantities, planting region, hires and
sales. This change does not introduce new rewards, an abandon/cancel action,
automatic housing for arbitrary reset-state stock, or persistent unpurchased
batches. Modes 0, 1 and 3 retain their previous execution contract. Existing
mode-2 weights retain compatible action IDs but should be reevaluated under
the changed executor, including frozen league policies.

Verification:

  Starting from $3,000 with one empty compatible structure and no cash top-ups,
  goose/cow/sheep survive and harvest 54 eggs / 36 milk / 34 wool respectively.
  No sales, further livestock purchases, or extra land/housing are synthesized.
- Mixed cow/goose/tomato fixture collects all three products while the policy
  requests daily labor; no sale action is selected.
- Early versus mature one-shot harvest and feeding during HARVEST are tested.
- All 20 portable-runtime fixtures pass.
- Native/portable mode-2 action parity covers both seats, legal macro choices
  and two quantities along rule and cow trajectories. Existing mode-3 parity
  also passes.
- Vast CPU/GPU transition parity passes 12 cases × 1,440 turns, including
  structured cow-production followed by HOLD with positive milk collection.
  State, observations, masks, rewards, resets and logs match.
