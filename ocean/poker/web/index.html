    NO-LIMIT TEXAS HOLD’EM

    How to play

    Make the best five-card hand using your two cards and the community cards. Win at showdown or make everyone else fold.

    Check / call
    Check when the bet is free, or call the amount needed to stay in.
    Bet / raise to
    The displayed amount is your total for this betting round, including chips you already put in. Preset buttons select a size; press Bet or Raise to commit it.
    Preflop presets
    3 BB / 5 BB are opening totals. Facing a raise, these become 3× / 5× the current bet. All sizes respect the legal minimum and your stack.
    Postflop presets
    ½ pot, ¾ pot and pot use the pot after your call. A pot raise adds that amount to the current bet. Each button shows the resulting legal total.
    Fold
    Leave the hand and forfeit committed chips. You can’t fold when checking is free.
    Your session
    Buy in for 200 BB. Stacks carry over and blinds rotate. Busted bots automatically rebuy for 200 BB; your rebuy button appears when you bust. Hands deal automatically after a short result pause. Pause the result to review it; the hand history keeps the winners and their shown cards. Automatic dealing pauses when this tab is hidden, or while Help, Settings or History is open. Refreshing restores this tab’s current hand and session. Practice chips only.
