    THE BASICS

    A good hand starts here.

    Make the best five-card hand using your two cards and the five community cards. Win at showdown, or make everyone else fold.

    Check / call
    Check when the bet is free, or call the amount needed to stay in.
    Bet / raise
    The amount shown is your total commitment on this betting round, including chips already in front of you.
    Fold
    Leave the hand and forfeit chips already committed. You can’t fold when checking is free.
