# Briar Court surface decals: placement (presentation only).
# Grammar: see surface_decals.h. Coordinates are engine metres (+Y up). A decal
# is snapped onto the outermost real surface within 8 cm behind / 3 cm in front
# of its plane and clipped to every coplanar wall section, slab or prop face it
# overlaps; breached sections take their decals with them.
# Layers draw in order: 0 broad grime, 1 stains/streaks, 2 rust/drips, 3 tags.
