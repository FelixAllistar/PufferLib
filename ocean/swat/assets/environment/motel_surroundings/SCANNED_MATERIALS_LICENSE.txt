1024 x 1024 PNG inputs; 2048 x 2048 originals are optional source-only inputs.

0.6499999762), roughnessFactor 1, metallicFactor 0. No AO or displacement is used.
Bark and leaves retain their original v1 512 x 512 maps and UVs. See unchanged
V2_README.md for the existing implementation, preservation checks and limits.
