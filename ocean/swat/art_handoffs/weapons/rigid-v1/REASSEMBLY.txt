Two-part lossless weapon handoff

Both parts are raw slices of the same original ZIP. They are not separate ZIPs.
Download the two .part files, parts_manifest.json and reassemble.py into one folder.
Run: python reassemble.py
The script checks both hashes, creates the original ZIP and checks its hash and CRC.
Then extract rigid-rifle7-textured-and-sidearm50-reference-v1.zip normally.

The rifle retains its original embedded 4096 x 4096 textures without degradation.
The full package includes both GLBs, measured bindings, validation, neutral views,
provenance/source hashes, small geometry references and extraction recipes.
The optional sidearm remains a clearly labeled source-scale reference.

Alternatively, concatenate part01 then part02 as raw bytes, and compare the
resulting SHA256 with parts_manifest.json. The original archive SHA256 is:
