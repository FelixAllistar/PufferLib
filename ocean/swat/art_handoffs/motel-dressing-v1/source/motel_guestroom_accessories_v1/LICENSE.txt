Textures: deterministic NumPy seed 672685, 512 x 512 PNG. Basecolor is sRGB; normal_gl is tangent-space OpenGL (+Y); roughmetal is linear R=255 (unused), G=roughness, B=metallic. Each object's AO is separately baked original geometry in Blender on uniquely packed UV1, linear. No baked lighting in basecolor. Scripts adapted from the original CC0 motel bathroom accessory build pipeline.
