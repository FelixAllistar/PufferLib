# Construction and source references

These references inform ordinary material and construction choices. The fictional assets do not reproduce a branded product, logo or commercial game environment.

## Air conditioner

GE's RAB71B engineering guide describes a folded/enamelled PTAC sleeve, rear grille, flange, support and drainage provisions. Its 42-inch format is a closer construction reference than a small household window unit. The candidate retains its existing 1.01 × .399 × .60m art envelope, so it is not a dimensionally accurate GE replica.

- https://www.geappliances.com/content/migrated-ge/zoneline/downloads/20-S0221.pdf
- https://www.geappliances.com/appliance/Zoneline-One-Piece-Wall-Sleeve-RAB71B

## Window and curtain

Milgard's aluminum horizontal-slider reference supplies overlapping sash/track/lock vocabulary. Hospitality pinch-pleat curtains support replacing individual box slats with continuous hung fabric. This compact existing window retains short recessed panels within its original envelope; no new opening or transparency is implied.

- https://www.milgard.com/windows/A250/horizontal-slider?field_resource_type_target_id=346
- https://www.hunterdouglashospitality.com/product_line/pinch-pleat/

## Bedding

Standard Textile's economical sheet range is a reference for simple plain textile layering and hem construction. The candidate is a simplified authored shape, not a copied product pattern or cloth simulation.

- https://express.standardtextile.com/products-sheets.php
