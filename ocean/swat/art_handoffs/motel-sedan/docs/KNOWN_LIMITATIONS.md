# Known limitations

This candidate passed the independent offline source, export, reproducibility, opaque-material and wheel-contact checks documented in `qa/independent/`. It remains a game-art candidate for engine integration.

Visual caveats accepted in this revision:

- The thin rear deck shut line is faint and partly disappears from some oblique views. This is small surface-detail visibility, not an opening in the body shell.
- Some wheel-cover vent slots appear uneven or partly occluded in the close wheel view. The tire, rim seating and wheel/arch clearances remain intact.
- The lower front valance can show bright pinched/faceted highlights under the strong neutral review lighting. There are no inverted normals, degenerate triangles or open edges; the highlight is a modest low-poly shading limitation.

Static exterior only. No transparent interior, animation, driving/physics, collision, damage, LODs, target-engine import, batching or target-hardware performance acceptance is supplied. The undertray, inner wheel dishes and axle hints are visual support only, not mechanically complete vehicle construction.
