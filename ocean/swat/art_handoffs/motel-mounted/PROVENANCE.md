# Provenance and scope

Created 2026-10-09 as a distinct source-art candidate for the motel/environment collection. The two recent-delivery catalog directories were checked before selection. Existing service meters and disconnect boxes were excluded from the design; no compact junction-box/conduit-fitting design was found there.

Geometry: original procedural meshes, manufactured primitives, real housing port cutouts, slotted screw cuts, hollow conduit and connector rings, and bent saddle cross sections. No imported mesh/reference was used. Assembly volumes are independent manufactured parts and can overlap at joins; there is no hidden cavity-fill geometry.

Material: original seeded 1024-pixel atlas generated by the included script: fine polygonal zinc spangle, restrained grey variation, subtle edge grime/oxide, fine scratches, roughness/metallic variation and subtle normal microdetail. No externally sourced image, scan, text, lettering, font, logo or brand appears. No external service was used to generate the art.

License: original included work is dedicated under CC0 1.0 as described in LICENSE.txt. Dependencies are not bundled.

Scope: source art and audit/preview files only. No installed placement or engine project was modified. Ground, character motion/grip and previously accepted assets are untouched. This package is a candidate, not a user-approved art replacement or a hardware-performance certification.
