# Briar Court road context

Installed native art uses the texture-wear 40-triangle paint, two original grass
models and 36 measured placements. Grass is soft, never ballistic cover. Each
placement inherits every existing support under its footprint; W_01 requires
1119 and 1120. Paint belongs to road owner 1123, stays 1 mm above its measured
surface and receives lighting without casting a shadow. No new world objects,
colliders or navigation obstacles are added. The 12 m road crossing stays clear.

The packed editable sources here preserve the art. The original high-triangle
paint and duplicate runtime assembly are retained in the complete Drive backup,
not loaded by the game. Provenance/license files accompany active runtime assets.
No authoring Python was executed locally.
