# conifers hill-climb log

Track: real, dense, tall conifers (dawn redwood, araucaria, podocarp, cheirolep cypress).
Each line: pass, the worst fault seen, the change, kept or reverted, and why.

Triangles at the start (full / reduced, from the gallery labels): redwood v2 15762 / 1976, v3 14729 / 1928, v7 15050 / 1964;
araucaria v0 12902 / 3546, v4 12684 / 3430; podocarp v1 37610 / 3748, v5 54966 / 4536; cheirolep v6 8442 / 992.

1. Fault: every conifer is half its real height (redwood 22-30 m, araucaria 15-23, podocarp 12-18, cheirolep 6-10). Change: mesh heights redwood 32-40 m, araucaria 26-34, podocarp 17-22, cheirolep 15-22 (instances scale 0.7-1.25 round that), trunk radius per metre of height lowered so girth stays about 1/25-1/35 of height. Kept: the brief's sizes; crowns grow with the height, the cypress and podocarp triangle counts with them (fixed in later passes).
2. Fault: the redwood sprays carry flat brown-green bars. Cause: the fir twig set's first cut is a strip of trunk bark (aspect 0.21), stamped into L_SPRAY and L_CL_SPRAY like a twig. Change: drop spray sprites narrower than 0.45 (render/veg.c photos_load). Kept: sprays read finer and feathery, the bars are gone, nothing else uses the set.
3. Fault: the dawn redwood is a stick tree: a bare pole with 0.6 m-spaced flat combs, sky through every gap. Change: gen_metasequoia rebuilt: branches every 0.3-0.5 m in a spiral, level low and rising high (narrow cone, base radius 0.17 H), opposite side shoots splayed in a shallow layer, sprays along shoots and outer branch and on the leader's last tenth (4700 cluster cards); the reduced mesh uses the 2x2 cluster layer whole as its merged card (four sprays per card) and only draws limbs over 4.5 cm. Kept: a dense layered cone you cannot see through; reduced and impostor keep the outline. Triangles v2 29742 / 2296 (budget 31524 / 2470).
4. Fault: the podocarp reads as a see-through broadleaf: an egg of thin twigs with scattered clusters (and 65k triangles). Change: gen_podocarp rebuilt: a leader with limbs every 0.3-0.5 m rising 17-50 degrees, each ending in one to three rounded foliage pads (leaf points biased to the pad surface, a few twigs into each), a full-then-rounding column (radius 0.27 H), a pad round the top; 7000 cluster cards; reduced mesh every seventh as a whole 2x2 cluster card. Kept: a dense, dark, lumpy column with shade between masses, like totara; triangles fell (v1 34352 / 2498, v5 34840 / 2566; were 37610 / 3748 and 54966 / 4536 at the old size). The impostor is a little darker than the full mesh (see later passes).
5. Fault: the cheirolep cypress is a 6-10 m open bush of crown_tree twigs, nothing like Cupressus or Taxodium. Change: the podocarp's pad crown made general (pad_conifer: width, limb elevations, pad shape and size, crown base, a pointed top sheathing the leader); the cypress is a narrow column (radius 0.11 H) of steep limbs with tall, narrow pads of scale-leaf rope clusters from a tenth of its height, narrowing to a rounded point, the trunk leaning up to 5 degrees. Tried and dropped on the way: shrinking the top limbs' pads (the crown came out an egg, shorter than the trunk). Kept: a dense flame-shaped column like an Italian cypress; 10324 / 1340 triangles, cards then cut to 2600 for the reduced budget (1240).
6. Fault: the Norfolk araucaria's tiers are single alternate rows of sprays (see-through fishbones), the crown is too wide (bottom branches 0.27 H) and the dead lowest branches hang to the ground like black wires. Change: opposite sprays at every 0.2 m step (one card each, rolled 25 degrees either way in turn), branches 0.2 H at the bottom, dead branches broken stubs a third as long; the reduced mesh lays whole pinnate fronds (L_ARAU) along each branch, 1.15 m apart and alternately tilted, instead of enlarged single sprays, has no tip cords and coarser cones. Kept: denser, narrower tiers with the right gaps, no wires; the reduced outline now matches the full one. v0 21110 / 3606, v4 20114 / 3542 triangles (budget 25804 / 4432; the reduced mesh was 5644 at the new height before this pass).
7. Fault: every variant of a species is the same tree at a different seed; no young or old trees (the brief wants ages). Change: veg_conifer_age (render/veg_place.h) gives each variant an age: redwood v3 young (17-23 m, clothed nearly to the ground, broad-based cone), v2 mature, v7 old (40-46 m, crown from a third up, broad rounded top, dead lower limbs as bare stubs); araucaria v4 old (34-40 m, bare trunk with stubs to nearly half its height, long upper tiers: a flat-topped crown); podocarp v5 old (20-23 m, a broad high crown on a tall bole). Redwood limbs thinner (they read as logs from under the crown), twig tubes only on shoots over 0.95 m, 5400 spray cards. Kept: the three ages read at a glance at all LODs. Triangles (full / reduced): redwood v2 25478 / 2324, v3 19166 / 1784, v7 25998 / 2436; araucaria v0 19862 / 3562, v4 19452 / 3328; podocarp v1 33768 / 2448, v5 35972 / 2550; cypress v6 9510 / 1224 (all within 2x / 1.25x).
8. Fault: the dawn redwood is as dark as a fir; Metasequoia is a fresh, light yellow-green, the lightest of the conifers. Change: spray layers' mean albedo (L_SPRAY, L_CL_SPRAY) from 0.045/0.098/0.036 to 0.08/0.16/0.036, and the redwood's crown occlusion constant 0.5 to 0.32 (its fine sprays let light in). Tried first: albedo alone (barely visible: the crown's baked shade dominates). Kept: lighter, warmer crowns, still shaded inside; nothing else uses the layers.
