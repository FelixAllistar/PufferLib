# near: hill-climb log (round 2)

Track: the trees you stand under (full meshes, near bark and leaf shading, the small woody plants).
One line per pass: the fault, the change, kept or reverted, why. Views: the gallery's match capture of
single trees at 3 and 10 m (eye 1.2 m) and from under the crown (`CRET_GALLERY_MATCH_AIM=0.6`,
`CRET_GALLERY_MATCH_EYE=1.0`, 4 and 9 m; the two switches were added for this pass), the tree gallery
pages, and island world shots.

1. Cycad trunks drew near black (sRGB 19 on the lit side at 3 m), tree-fern and palm trunks nearly so, all under their own crown. Three causes, three changes: bark under a low crown is now baked by the visibility of its sides, a cell and a half out from its surface (it counted the trunk's own area and the crown above as occluders); bark takes twice the leaves' ground bounce (terrain.fs takes about three times; a trunk's sides face the lit ground); the cycad, palm and tree-fern bark tones are lighter (cycad 0.11 to 0.17 albedo, warm brown). Kept: the cycad trunk 19 to 38 sRGB with its armour visible, the palm a grey ringed column, other trunks in shade a little warmer and less blue.
2. The chloranth (understorey tree, 3.5-5.5 m) was a narrow see-through bush, a head of oversized leaf clusters on bare stems. Change: 980 cards (520) of 0.3 m (0.38), clump 0.9, 3-4 stems from lower down, a broader, taller dome (spread 1.12, was 0.95); the reduced mesh keeps its card count (its divisor scaled with the cards). Kept: at 10 m and from under it, a full rounded shrubby crown on several stems; the reduced mesh at 30 m still matches it.
3. The plane's mottling was cold teal-white and smeared (the grey-green and cream, under the blue sky fill). Change: a warm palette (grey-brown/fawn outer bark, khaki-olive, cream-yellow fresh bark) and the fresh patches 60% smoothed toward the scan's mean, so the furrows stay on the old bark. Kept: at 3 m and from under the crown the trunk and limbs read as a plane's warm jigsaw camouflage.
4. No trunk bowed (round 1 reverted a lean because moving the trunk reshuffled the crown's random draws). Change: `mesh_bow` shears the finished mesh by a smooth sweep plus a gentler S over the bottom two thirds of its height, back to zero above, from its own seed: no crown draw changes and the full and reduced meshes bow alike. Broadleaves 4.5% of that height (the chloranth 6%), podocarp and cypress 2.8%, redwood and araucarias 1.2%. A first try at half that was invisible at 15 m. Kept: the plane and ginkgo trunks curve visibly at 15 m, the others a little; the foot does not move (colliders unchanged).
5. From under a crown the leaves were flat dark cut-outs: their lower faces only saw the dark ground. Change (veg_light.glsl): leaves seen from below (meshes only, not impostors or ground cover) add the sky light let through the blade from their upper face, yellow-green, scaled by the crown's depth. A first try with the depth squared made no visible change. Kept: a modest, visible lift on the lower, outer leaves of the laurel and magnolia from 3 m; the deep inside of dense crowns stays dark, as it should (skylight through a leaf is dim; the bright glow is the sun's, already there).
6. From under the magnolia its leaves were 40-50 cm sheets (0.62 m cluster cards; Magnolia grandiflora's leaves are 15-25 cm). Change: cards 0.46 m, 1600 (1000) of them; the reduced mesh's divisor scaled so it keeps its card count. Kept: from 3 m below, a leafy layered canopy instead of big flat sheets; at 15 m the crown is as full as before, and the reduced crown at 40 m unchanged.
7. Two faults. The cycads' dead fronds hung as black wire fishbones (14 pairs of thin leaflets): now 24 pairs of longer, wider leaflets folded down, a withered frond. And the world shots showed pass 1's doubled bark bounce made the redwoods and tree ferns in the forest paler and pinker than before: the extra bounce now goes only to cycad and palm trunks (2x) and tree-fern trunks (1.6x), set per bark tone in veg.fs, and the tree-fern bark tone is back to 1. Kept: dead fronds read as a dry skirt at 3 m; in `redwood` and `vf_forest` the forest trunks match the round-1 build again, and the cycad trunks keep pass 1's gain.
8. The squat cycad (Zamia, Dioon) read as a pot plant: an upright vase of short fronds on a straight-sided barrel. Change: fronds 15% longer, leaving lower (0.3-1.1 rad, was 0.55-1.15) and drooping more, a broad arching rosette; the trunk swells at the foot and tapers into the rosette, a dome of leaf bases. Same frond and draw counts, so the reduced mesh stays the same plant. Kept: at 3 and 10 m both squat cycads read as low, broad cycads.
9. The gunnera was a scrap: 4-7 thin stalks with leaves well under a metre across, lying near edge on (Gunnera: a clump of leaves up to 1.5-2 m across on stout stalks). Change: stalks 1.2-2.1 m (0.7-1.4) and thicker, blades 0.8-1.15 (0.55-0.85) tilted up and out like a shallow dish and centred over the stalk's end, and 3-5 more inner leaves on the full mesh only (own stream, 4-sided stalks, to stay in budget). A first try (bigger, but blades still level and outward) stayed sparse and edge on from eye height. Kept: at 4 and 12 m a full clump of big lobed leaves.
