# Broadleaf hill-climb log

One line per pass: the worst fault seen, the change, kept or reverted, and why.

1. Fault: broadleaf trees were 5 to 14 m (magnolia 8-13, laurel 7-11, katsura 9-14, plane 12-17, ginkgo 10-15), bushes next to the conifers. Change: real heights (ginkgo 16-22 m, katsura 13-18, laurel 9-13, plane 21-27, magnolia 11-16, chloranth 3.5-5.5; trunk girth about 1/25-1/40 of the height), and `crown_scale` grows each crown's twig spacing, cluster size and card count with the tree so a tall crown is not sparser, the reduced mesh keeping as many (longer) cards. Kept: the trees now stand at tree height in the gallery; crowns still read sparse (next pass).
2. Fault: crowns were sparse sprigs strung along the skeleton, lopsided on the plane. Change: `Crown.clump`, a domed mass of leaf points round the outer part of each branch (open below), so foliage gathers in clouds with shade between. Kept: laurel and magnolia read as round full crowns, the plane's crown fills both sides.
3. Fault: the full mesh's crown was see-through next to its reduced mesh, and two thirds of its triangles were 3-sided twig tubes hidden inside the crown. Change: `crown_full` draws every other twig and spends the triangles on 1.5 times the leaf cards (the reduced mesh keeps its card count). Kept: denser full crowns at the same cost.
4. Fault: cycads were knee to head high and tree ferns 3-7 m (the stout one 2-4 m): bushes on a stub. Change: Cycas trunks 0.9-2.2 m and 2.2-3.8 m (the tall form), squat cycads and Williamsonia a little taller, Cyathea trunks 4.5-8 m and Dicksonia 2.2-4.2 m, and tree-fern fronds a little longer on the taller trunks. Kept: they stand as small trees in the gallery; the tree-fern crowns still read as a dark dome (next).
5. Fault: tree-fern fronds bent back on themselves (tip angle e0 - 1.5 x droop reached -2.6 rad), so every crown was a tight dark mop. Change: droop 0.8-1.05 (was 1.4-2.1) and the oldest fronds leave lower: fronds rise, run out to level and drop their tips. Kept: an open spreading rosette, the stout fern a shallow vase.
6. Fault: tree-fern fronds read as fat paddles. Change: fronds 15% longer with pinnae at 0.32 of the frond (was 0.4). Reverted: barely visible at 15 m, not clearly better.
7. Fault: the reduced mesh drew the taller crowns with 2-3 m merged cards (giant leaves at 30-45 m) and spent most of its triangles on branches hidden in the crown (over its 1.25x budget). Change: merged cards k^0.6 longer and k^0.8 as many (k = height over the tuned height), and the reduced mesh drops branches under 2.5 cm x height/10 instead of 2.5 cm. Kept: finer, fuller reduced crowns at true leaf scale, every reduced broadleaf within 1.25x of its old count.
